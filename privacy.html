Paper Trail Privacy Policy

Policy version: 1.1 · Effective: October 26, 2026 · Contact: support.papertrail@gmail.com

Paper Trail is an independent budgeting app ("we", "us" means the person who builds and runs it). This policy says, in plain English, what the app keeps, what leaves your device, and what you can do about it.

1. The short version

  • Your books live on your device. You don't need an account.
  • If you create an account, a copy of your books is backed up to your private cloud account. Vault files stay on your device.
  • Only invited testers who type in a tester code share app data. Without a code, nothing is sent. Dollar amounts and Vault details are off unless a tester turns them on.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your data.
  • You can export or erase everything at any time.

2. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Paper Trail web app at https://lukemartinson2000-coder.github.io/paper-trail/ and any copy of it installed on your home screen.

3. What stays on your device

Paper Trail stores your data in your browser's storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) on this device: - Budget: months, envelopes, planned amounts. - Transactions: expenses, income, refunds, splits, merchant names, notes. - Income: paychecks, income statement, confirmed pay-stub numbers. - Net worth: yearly balances and notes. - Vault documents: files you add and the details on each card. - Settings: name, birthday, ZIP, pay frequency, dark mode, plan, setup checklist. - Automatic backup copies on the device, so you can recover from a bad save.

Pay stubs and documents are read on your device. When you add a pay stub or document, the app reads it in your browser. The file is not uploaded for processing.

If your device is lost or the browser data is cleared and you have no backup, we can't recover the data. Save a backup now and then (Profile › Data).

4. Optional account and cloud backup

An account is optional. If you create one: - Sign-in: your email and password are handled by our hosting provider, Supabase (see §8). We never see your password. Every sign-in also asks for a 6-digit code sent to your email (two-step sign-in). Our database checks this on the server: a session that signed in with only a password can't read, change, or delete your backups, so a password alone can't open your account. Because the code goes to your email, keep your email account secure too. - What's backed up: your saved books, including the details on each Vault card. The Vault files themselves are not uploaded. Each backup also records the time, device ID, app version, transaction count and size. - When: when you open the app and a few seconds after each change. You can pause backup on a device. - How many: the server keeps your last 10 versions. - Who can read it: row-level security means your account can read only its own rows. We don't look at your books except to fix a problem you report to us, and only with your permission. - Two devices: if a backup from another device differs from this one, the app asks which to keep. It never overwrites either side on its own.

5. Tester data sharing

Nothing is shared unless you type in a tester code we gave you (like PT-001). If you do, the app sends data tied to that code to a private Google Sheet that only we can see. The data goes to a Google Apps Script web app that accepts only tester codes we've listed.

What's shared
Shared once you enter a tester code Savings rates, average tax, tax brackets, year-over-year changes, plan vs. spent %, each envelope's share of spending (numbered, never named), your grade, and how often you use features
Off unless you turn it on (Profile › Data sharing) Income dollar amounts, other dollar amounts, and Vault details (number of documents and their types, not the files)
Never shared Envelope names, merchant names, transaction notes, account numbers, file contents, your name, phone, birthday, and ZIP

You can preview exactly what's shared in Profile › Data sharing.

6. No surveys

Paper Trail doesn't ask you survey questions. Your pay frequency and take-home pay, if you add them, stay in your books on your device.

7. No ads, no tracking, no selling

  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party scripts. The app's code and fonts are served with the app.
  • No tracking cookies. The app uses browser storage only to run itself and to keep you signed in.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we never share it for advertising.

8. Services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app files. Like any web host, it may log your IP address and browser when you load the page.
  • Supabase runs accounts and cloud backup (only if you create an account).
  • Google (Apps Script and Sheets) receives tester data (only if you have a tester code).

9. Bank linking and automatic tracking (not yet)

Paper Trail does not connect to your bank today. We plan to add: - Apple Pay tracking through an iPhone Shortcut you set up yourself. - Bank linking through Plaid.

Before either one goes live, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent. Neither will be turned on without you choosing it.

10. Your choices: export and delete

  • Export: an Excel workbook, a monthly PDF report, or a full backup file you can restore later (Profile › Data).
  • Delete on device: "Erase all data" in the danger zone at the bottom of Profile removes your books and the backup copies on this device.
  • Delete in the cloud: when you erase while signed in, the app offers to delete your cloud backups too.
  • Delete your account: Profile › Account › Delete my account. We email you a 6-digit code to confirm, then your account and every cloud backup are deleted. You can also email support.papertrail@gmail.com and we'll do it for you.
  • Tester data: email support.papertrail@gmail.com with your tester code and we'll delete your rows from the tester sheet. Clear your tester code or switch dollar sharing off anytime in Profile › Data sharing.

11. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Row-level security protects cloud backups. Only the public key is in the app.
  • Two-step sign-in (a code emailed to you) is required for every account and enforced by our server, not just the app. After a password reset, you sign in again with your new password and a new code.

No system is perfectly secure. Use a strong password and keep your device locked. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we'll tell you.

12. Age requirement

Paper Trail is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

13. Not financial or tax advice

Grades, tax estimates, payoff dates and projections are estimates for education only. They aren't financial, tax, investment or legal advice. Check important decisions with a qualified professional.

14. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the version number and date at the top. For meaningful changes, the app will ask you to read and agree again before you keep using it.

15. Contact

Questions or requests: support.papertrail@gmail.com.
